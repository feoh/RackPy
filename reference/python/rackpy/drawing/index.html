rackpy.drawing¶

Pythonic drawing for widgets’ draw() callbacks, over rackpy.raw.nanovg. Inside Rack only.

Painter(args) wraps the callback’s GraphicsContext and expires with it. Colours are an NVGcolor, a "#RRGGBB"/"#RRGGBBAA" string, or an (r, g, b)/(r, g, b, a) tuple of 0-1 floats. Fonts and images are loaded through Rack’s window on every call, as Rack advises: its caches make that cheap, and they expire with the window. Everything here is the raw layer’s behaviour in another shape; nothing has native semantics of its own.
