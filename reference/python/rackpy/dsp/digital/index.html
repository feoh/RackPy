rackpy.dsp.digital¶

Rack’s triggers, pulse generator, timers and clock divider (RP-18B).

Rack’s own objects (rack::dsp, also available as rackpy.raw.dsp), each holding its C++ state, so they behave exactly as in a Rack module. For vectors of four, subscript the templates: TSchmittTrigger[float_4], TTimer[float_4]. For testing and offline processing; per-sample audio belongs in a native kernel. Available inside Rack only.
